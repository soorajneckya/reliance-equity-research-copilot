# Memo: Chain-of-Thought vs Tree-of-Thoughts

Use structured stepwise analysis for bounded tasks such as interpreting five financial ratios. Require formulas, inputs, reporting period/basis, supporting and counter-evidence, missing data, and a concise rationale. Calculate ratios deterministically in Python rather than asking a language model to guess.

Use scenario branching for uncertain multi-year outcomes. Compare bull, base, and bear cases over three to five years, stating assumptions, evidence, financial implications, risks, and monitorable indicators. Prune unsupported branches against explicit criteria.

Selection: structured ratio analysis for fundamental health; scenario branching for long-term rating; self-consistency for instability detection. Split votes, low confidence, and missing audited inputs must be escalated. Do not expose private hidden chain-of-thought; retain a concise audit trail of sources, inputs, formulas, outputs, confidence, missing-data flags, and analyst decisions.

Every output: Educational research support only; not investment advice.
